Our Services

Comprehensive Pediatric Care

We offer a wide range of healthcare services tailored specifically for children of all ages.

Newborn Care

Specialized care for newborns including routine check-ups, health screenings, and early developmental assessments.

Vaccination

Complete immunization programs following national schedules to protect your child from preventable diseases.

General Pediatrics

Comprehensive care for common childhood illnesses including fever, cold, cough, infections, and allergies.

Growth Monitoring

Regular tracking of your child's physical growth and developmental milestones to ensure healthy progress.

Respiratory Care

Treatment for asthma, bronchitis, pneumonia, and other respiratory conditions with nebulization support.

Nutrition Counseling

Expert dietary guidance for children to ensure proper nutrition and address feeding-related concerns.
